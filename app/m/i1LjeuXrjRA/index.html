What it means: The Boston City Council Committee on Labor, Prevention, and Economic Development held a hearing on Docket 1374 regarding first responder health and wellness screenings [4:26], [5:23]. City administration officials noted that city health insurance covers routine preventive screenings at no cost [13:10], [13:20] and stated that added departmental benefits are traditionally negotiated through collective bargaining [36:00], [44:13], [51:42].
