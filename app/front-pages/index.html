Front pages

The record presses its own every night. Readers press theirs whenever they like and share them as links. Nobody signs — the record keeps no names — so a front page is judged by its receipts: every chart, reel and bracket points back into the tape. The ask to take a reader’s page down is on the page itself; a steward decides.
