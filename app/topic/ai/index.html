How Boston talks about AI

The first time anyone said “AI” on Boston’s record was July 8, 2026, 4:08:14 into a City Council meeting: “LATEST MODEL OF ACTOR TROPIC GENERATIVE AI TOOL. THE INITIATIVE WOULD HELP TO”. The next meeting passed without it. It peaked on October 19, 2026, when the City Council Committee said it 21 times over 1 h 57 min — 25% of every mention on the record. In all, 84 mentions across 11 of Boston’s 42 meetings, by the City Council Committee, the City Council and the School Committee; the words said beside it most: data, literacy, centers, models. The latest was October 20, 2026: “REPORTING THAT IF SOMETHING IS ON SOCIAL MEDIA OR AI OR SOMETHING'S FLOATING AROUND OR”. Brookline’s bodies said it in 78 lines across 13 meetings — the whole record’s search reads them together.
