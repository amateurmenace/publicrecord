Search the record

Search reads the record two ways at once — the words you typed, and what they mean. Nothing about you is sent with the query.

  1. Search a word. A program, a street, a worry. Three letters is enough — the record searches as you type. Every hit is one line of one transcript, with its time.
  2. See how it was said. The record counts every line that says it and draws the count: month by month, night by night, the words beside it. Nothing modeled; every number opens the tape.
  3. Cut it, share it. Press ▶ play all and the hits play as a reel. Press ＋ reel on any hit to cut your own. The link is the share — no account, nothing uploaded.
